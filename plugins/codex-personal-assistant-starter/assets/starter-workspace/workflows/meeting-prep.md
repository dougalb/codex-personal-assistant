# Meeting Prep Workflow

## Goal

Prepare for one meeting using approved local or connected sources.

## Steps

1. Identify the meeting, attendees, and purpose.
2. Gather source material the user approves.
3. Separate facts from assumptions.
4. Draft agenda, likely decisions, questions, and follow-ups.
5. Save the result in `outputs/YYYY-MM-DD-meeting-prep.md`.
